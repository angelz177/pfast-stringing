Good to know

Questions, answered.

How fast is "usually within 24 hours"?

Most racquets are strung the same evening they're dropped off and ready the next day. If something's going to take longer, I'll tell you before you drop it off.

Can you do it today? I have a match.

Often, yes. Pick "Emergency" on the form and I'll let you know right away whether I can fit it in. It depends on what's already on the bench.

What tension should I ask for?

If you're not sure, say "you pick." I'll use the middle of your frame's recommended range (printed on the throat), a bit lower for full poly, and adjust from there next time based on how it felt.

Where do I drop off?

Anywhere around Weiss High School, Hendrickson High School or Pflugerville High School, or in between. I'll suggest a convenient spot and a drop-off window in my reply so we're not guessing at each other's schedules. Pickup works the same way.
